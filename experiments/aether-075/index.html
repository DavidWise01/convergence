The calibrator uses a known 40-sample excitation, 23 training samples (indices 3–25), 12 validation samples (28–39), and candidate integer lags 0,1,2. Certificates last through the following epoch, with renewal every second epoch. Residual monitoring uses the same known challenge sequence and therefore is an explicit calibration check, not independent field evidence. Gain and lag truth are retained in the audit only, for scoring. An unexpired certificate can still be inaccurate below the chosen 0.0005 residual threshold. The synthetic references are not independent witnesses; neither fusion nor fundamental-force physics is simulated.
